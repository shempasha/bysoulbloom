Good To Know

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to begin planning your custom celebration or installation.

What services do you offer?

Wedding floral styling, ceremony and reception design, proposals, bridal and baby showers, birthday celebrations, tablescapes and seasonal home installations. We do not offer standalone retail flower bouquets; floral design is part of our event and space styling.

How much does wedding design cost?

Wedding design projects begin at $2,500. Every wedding is customized, so final pricing depends on the scale, florals, rentals, materials, installation and logistics.

What are your Christmas decorating prices?

Holiday wreaths begin at $150; fireplace or mantel styling at $500; stairs at $350; table decorations at $500; front porch or entry at $500; Christmas tree styling at $449. These are starting prices, not flat-rate quotes.

How do I reserve a date?

Send an inquiry with your date, location, services and inspiration. After confirming availability, we provide a personalized proposal. Wedding bookings require a 25% non-refundable deposit.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, please reach out at least one month in advance whenever possible. Earlier is recommended for large events and peak seasonal dates. Other projects depend on our availability.

Where do you work?

Our base is the Greater Philadelphia area. We generally serve locations within approximately 1.5–2 hours, with travel and delivery priced according to distance and project scope.

Do I keep the decorations?

Most decorative structures and reusable materials belong to By.Soulbloom and are collected after the event or rental period. Any items you will keep will be specified in your proposal.

Are setup and breakdown included?

Setup, breakdown, travel and other logistics are quoted separately as appropriate to the project. Your proposal will explain what is included.

Can you match my colors and theme?

Yes. We design around your preferred colors, seasonal aesthetic, inspiration and the space. Flowers and specific materials are subject to availability.

Do you decorate fireplaces, stairs and entrances?

Yes. Fireplace/mantel installations begin at $500, staircases at $350, front porch or entry designs at $500, and decorated tables at $500.

Can I book a romantic dinner or proposal setup?

Absolutely. We design romantic settings for Valentine’s Day, proposals, anniversaries and other meaningful occasions. Pricing is customized.

What if my event is outside?

We can style outdoor areas when access and conditions allow. Work requiring roof access, climbing or electrical installation is not offered.
