Questions

Everything you need to know.

What kinds of events and spaces do you style?

Weddings, intimate dinners, proposals, engagement parties, bridal and baby showers, birthdays, event tablescapes, seasonal decorations and custom floral installations. We do not offer bouquets as a standalone retail service.

How much does wedding styling cost?

Wedding design starts at $2,500. Each wedding receives a custom proposal based on the design, florals, venue, materials and installation.

How much does Christmas decorating cost?

Holiday wreaths start at $150; fireplace or mantel styling at $500; staircase decorating at $350; table decorations at $500; front porch and entry at $500; Christmas tree styling at $449. Final prices depend on the scope of your design.

How do I book a wedding or an event?

Send an event inquiry with the date, location, type of event, vision and budget. After confirming availability, we will prepare a custom proposal. Weddings require a 25% non-refundable booking deposit.

How far in advance should I reach out?

For weddings, ideally at least one month ahead. Earlier notice is best for larger events and seasonal installations. Availability varies.

Where do you work?

We serve Greater Philadelphia and surrounding locations typically within 1.5–2 hours. Travel fees may apply based on distance.

Are installation and breakdown included?

Setup, breakdown, travel and delivery are quoted separately depending on the project and will be outlined in your proposal.

Do I keep the decor after the event?

Our reusable decor and structures are normally provided for the event and reclaimed afterward. Your proposal will clarify which items, if any, are yours to keep.

Can you decorate a fireplace, staircase, table or front entrance?

Yes. Fireplace and mantel decor starts at $500, stairs at $350, table decoration at $500 and front porch or entry styling at $500.

Do you offer Valentine's or romantic proposals?

Yes. We create intimate dinner settings, proposal installations, anniversary and Valentine's Day decor customized to your celebration.

Are there any projects you do not offer?

We do not perform work requiring roof access, unsafe climbing, or electrical installation. Other decor and floral concepts are scoped individually.
